Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GAGE DE LAVEL, the developer and operator of "Let's move the car together" (the "Game"), handles information when the Game is used as a TikTok Mini Game.

1. Information handled by the Game

The Game does not require you to create a separate developer-operated account.

The Game may handle the following limited information:

  • Game progress, settings, rewards, virtual tickets, and similar gameplay state.
  • Gameplay events, app version, device category, performance, and crash diagnostics when analytics is enabled.
  • Rewarded-ad requests, ad completion status, and reward-delivery status when you choose to use an ad-supported feature.

Game progress and settings are primarily stored locally on your device or through storage provided by the TikTok Mini Game environment. The developer does not currently operate a separate cloud account or cloud-save service for the Game.

2. TikTok and service providers

TikTok hosts the Game and may process platform account information, device and network information, client identifiers, security signals, diagnostics, and usage information in accordance with TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

Rewarded advertising and analytics, if enabled in the released version, may be provided by TikTok or approved third-party providers. Those providers may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, usage events, and diagnostics under their own privacy policies. The developer does not sell personal information.

3. Permissions and sensitive data

The Game is not designed to request your contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or camera. If a future feature requires an additional permission, the Game will request it through the platform and this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

4. How information is used

  • To run gameplay, save progress, and deliver earned rewards.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, duplicated rewards, and technical errors.
  • To measure stability, diagnose crashes, and improve level balance and performance.
  • To comply with applicable law and platform requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally stored Game data remains until it is cleared by the platform, the Game is removed, or the device storage is reset. Data controlled by TikTok or another provider is retained according to that provider's policy. Because the Game does not currently maintain a separate user account database, the developer may be unable to identify or delete platform-controlled data directly.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children below the minimum age required by TikTok in their region. Access and age eligibility are also governed by TikTok's platform rules.

7. Security and international processing

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used for information under the developer's control. TikTok and other providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Game, SDK providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact GAGE DE LAVEL, the developer and operator, through github.com/wliang3266-bit.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 9 日

本隐私政策说明《Let's move the car together》（以下简称“本游戏”）的开发和运营主体 GAGE DE LAVEL 在本游戏作为 TikTok 小游戏运行时如何处理相关信息。

1. 本游戏处理的信息

本游戏不要求您创建由开发者独立运营的账号。

本游戏可能处理以下有限信息：

  • 游戏进度、设置、奖励、虚拟票券及类似游戏状态。
  • 启用分析功能时的游戏事件、应用版本、设备类别、性能及崩溃诊断信息。
  • 您主动使用广告功能时的激励广告请求、广告完成状态及奖励发放状态。

游戏进度和设置主要保存在您的设备本地或 TikTok 小游戏环境提供的存储中。 开发者目前不为本游戏运营独立云账号或云存档服务。

2. TikTok 与服务提供商

TikTok 为本游戏提供运行环境，并可能依据其自身条款和隐私政策处理平台账号信息、 设备与网络信息、客户端标识、安全信号、诊断及使用信息。

正式版本如启用激励广告或分析能力，相关服务可能由 TikTok 或获准的第三方提供。 这些服务商可能依据其自身隐私政策处理设备标识、由网络信息推断的大致位置、广告互动、 使用事件和诊断信息。开发者不会出售个人信息。

3. 权限与敏感数据

本游戏并非为获取您的通讯录、精确位置、照片、麦克风或摄像头权限而设计。 如未来功能需要新增权限，本游戏将通过平台发起权限请求，并在功能上线前更新本政策。

4. 信息用途

  • 运行游戏、保存进度并发放已获得的奖励。
  • 防止欺诈、滥用、奖励重复发放和技术错误。
  • 评估稳定性、诊断崩溃并改进关卡平衡和性能。
  • 遵守适用法律及平台要求。

5. 保存与删除

本地游戏数据会保留至平台清除数据、移除本游戏或设备存储被重置。由 TikTok 或其他 服务商控制的数据按相应服务商的政策保存。由于本游戏目前没有独立用户账号数据库， 开发者可能无法直接识别或删除由平台控制的数据。

6. 未成年人

本游戏不面向未达到其所在地区 TikTok 最低使用年龄的儿童。访问资格和年龄要求同时 受 TikTok 平台规则约束。

7. 安全与跨境处理

对开发者控制的信息会采取合理的技术和组织保护措施。TikTok 及其他服务商可能根据 其政策和适用法律在您所在国家或地区之外处理信息。

8. 政策更新

当游戏功能、SDK 服务商或法律要求发生变化时，本政策可能更新。重大变化会通过更新 本页面的生效日期予以说明。
